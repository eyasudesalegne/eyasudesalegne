## About me

I am a **PhD researcher in Biomedical Engineering at Istanbul University–Cerrahpaşa**, working across **computational neuroscience, neuroengineering, machine learning for healthcare, and research software engineering**. My work is motivated by a practical question: **how can computational models and AI systems produce evidence that remains interpretable, reproducible, and reliable beyond a single experiment or dataset?**

My research spans **EEG and TMS–EEG**, biophysical brain-network modeling, subject-specific neurostimulation simulation, retinal-image analysis, medical computer vision, and research-grade software infrastructure. I also work on applied biomedical engineering and R&D problems, connecting scientific methods with real-world engineering constraints.

I value **transparent evaluation, external validation, rigorous data separation, uncertainty reporting, and reproducibility** over headline accuracy alone.

## Scientific approach

```text
Clinical / scientific question
         ↓
Data provenance & quality control
         ↓
Mechanistic modeling or ML baseline
         ↓
Reproducible experimentation
         ↓
External / out-of-distribution evaluation
         ↓
Interpretability, uncertainty & failure analysis
         ↓
Evidence for the next research decision
```

In research software, I prioritize **traceable experiments, documented assumptions, locked evaluation protocols, explicit implementation status and honest limitations**.
